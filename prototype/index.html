C. Aganglionic Megacolon (Hirschsprung's Disease)
D. Midgut Malrotation with Volvulus
✅ Clinical Rationale: Duodenal Atresia results from failure of recanalization of the embryonic duodenum at weeks 8–10. The radiographic 'double bubble' sign represents gas in the distended stomach and proximal duodenal bulb. Over 30% of cases are associated with Trisomy 21. Contrast with Pyloric Stenosis which presents at 3–6 weeks with non-bilious projectile vomiting. — Nelson Textbook of Pediatrics, 21st Ed.
